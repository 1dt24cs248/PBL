# Energy experiment notes

The CSVs in `backend/experiments/out/` are generated by `python -m app.experiments.energy`; do not edit their rows by hand. The manifest records software versions, settings, tariff, period length, provenance notes, deterministic/random seed policy, and UTC timestamp.

## Assumptions and limits

- The requested source summary says **317 of 463 sessions use inferred rooms (the inheritance rule)**. The currently checked-in parser and seeded database were measured separately at **323 of 475**; this discrepancy is visible and is not silently reconciled.
- Source timetable clashes exist. In particular, fixed explicit lab sessions can double-book rooms. The validator permits untouched fixed-vs-fixed source conflicts so proposals can report them.
- Wattages and the Rs 8.50/kWh tariff are `SYNTHETIC`; energy values are equipment-derived estimates, not meter readings.
- SQLite is the verified development/test database. PostgreSQL behavior is unverified.
- E1, E2, E3, E5, and E6 are deterministic with CP-SAT configured for one worker and fixed seed 0. CLI `--seeds` is accepted for compatibility, but is not used to imply independent trials. Randomness exists only in E4, with seeds derived separately for each `(k, run)`.
- E3 runs every closure with both `lock_labs=true` and `lock_labs=false`. A locked lab assigned to a closure is reported as unable to move; when labs are unlocked, per-session reasons report destination eligibility/capacity failure or joint room-slot infeasibility. Constraints are not relaxed.
- E4 `relocations_extra` is the signed difference between relocations with a failure and relocations in the same run's no-failure baseline. `extra_students_affected` is `max(0, affected_students_with_failure - affected_students_without_failure)`.
- E5's earlier strict globally clash-free comparison was infeasible because fixed source room clashes remain. E6 uses `no_new_clashes`: new room clashes are those not present among room-fixed sessions in the source assignment. Faculty and section clashes are invariant under room-only reassignment, so source faculty/section conflicts are counted among fixed conflicts and cannot become new through a room change. The original inferred assignment is a reference row, not treated as a clash-comparable baseline when it has new clashes.
- E6 variants are inferred-room theory only, all theory, and all theory plus labs with lab locking disabled. Strategies are deterministic greedy first-fit, CP-SAT minimum-relocation, CP-SAT energy-aware, and energy-aware CP-SAT with relocation budgets 0, 5, 10, and 20. Every produced assignment is independently validated. A comparison is clash-comparable only when both assignments pass validation; energy weights are not retuned to produce a preferred result.
- E6 weight sensitivity reports the weighted energy, relocation, and floor objective terms so an unchanged assignment can be distinguished from a missing or constant energy term.
- The flat baseline assumes five days at six hours/day; timetable energy follows occupied periods, while utilization uses a six-day denominator.
- There is no real DSATM meter data, occupancy-sensor data, or operational room-status history. Results do not claim research novelty and report measured outputs without broader interpretation.

## Threats to validity

The inferred room assignments affect occupancy, energy, and the optimizer's movable set. The source schedule includes conflicts and possible parser ambiguities. Candidate availability, synthetic connected loads, the placeholder tariff, section strengths, and the assumed period/day model all affect results. SQLite-only verification means PostgreSQL deployment behavior remains unverified. Solver status and time are reported as produced; infeasibility is not hidden by changing constraints.
