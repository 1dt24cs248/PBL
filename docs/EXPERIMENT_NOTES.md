# Energy experiment notes

The CSVs in `backend/experiments/out/` are generated by `python -m app.experiments.energy`; do not edit their rows by hand. The manifest records software versions, settings, tariff, period length, provenance notes, deterministic/random seed policy, and UTC timestamp.

## Assumptions and limits

- The requested source summary says **317 of 463 sessions use inferred rooms (the inheritance rule)**. The currently checked-in parser and seeded database were measured separately at **323 of 475**; this discrepancy is reported as a dataset/parser count mismatch and is not silently reconciled.
- Source timetable clashes exist. In particular, fixed explicit lab sessions can double-book rooms. The validator permits untouched fixed-vs-fixed source conflicts so proposals can report them; those conflicts prevent a globally clash-free E5 comparison when no permitted movable assignment can clear them.
- Wattages and the Rs 8.50/kWh tariff are `SYNTHETIC`; energy values are equipment-derived estimates, not meter readings.
- SQLite is the verified development/test database. PostgreSQL behavior is unverified.
- E1, E2, E3, and E5 are deterministic with CP-SAT configured for one worker and fixed seed 0. CLI `--seeds` is accepted for compatibility, but is not used to imply independent trials. Randomness exists only in E4, with seeds derived separately for each `(k, run)`.
- E3 closures make every room-required session assigned to a closed room/floor movable and forbid closed resources as destinations. Locked labs in a closure make the model infeasible with a reason; constraints are not relaxed.
- E4 `relocations_extra` is the signed difference between relocations with a failure and relocations in the same run's no-failure baseline. `extra_students_affected` is `max(0, affected_students_with_failure - affected_students_without_failure)`.
- E5 compares greedy first-fit, CP-SAT with zero energy weight and minimum relocations, and energy-aware CP-SAT under the same movable set and hard constraints. A source-clash baseline is labelled `baseline_with_clashes_not_comparable`. It is not compared as if it were clash-free.
- The flat baseline assumes five days at six hours/day; timetable energy follows occupied periods, while utilization uses a six-day denominator.
- There is no real DSATM meter data, occupancy-sensor data, or operational room-status history. Results do not claim research novelty and report measured outputs without broader interpretation.

## Threats to validity

The inferred room assignments affect occupancy, energy, and the optimizer's movable set. The source schedule includes conflicts and possible parser ambiguities. Candidate availability, synthetic connected loads, the placeholder tariff, section strengths, and the assumed period/day model all affect results. SQLite-only verification means PostgreSQL deployment behavior remains unverified. Solver status and time are reported as produced; infeasibility is not hidden by changing constraints.
