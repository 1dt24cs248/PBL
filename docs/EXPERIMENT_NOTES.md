# Energy optimization experiment notes

The CSVs under `backend/experiments/out/` are generated by `python -m app.experiments.energy`; do not edit their rows by hand. The script records the input seeds, solver and Python versions, settings, tariff, period length, data-status notes, and UTC timestamp in `manifest.json`.

## Assumptions

- Equipment wattages and the Rs 8.50/kWh tariff are synthetic estimates. Room energy is calculated from equipment rows, not measured building meters.
- A scheduled room uses its connected equipment load for each occupied period. Period length is configurable and defaults to 55 minutes; unoccupied standby use defaults to zero.
- `inferred_only` uses imported sessions marked with an inferred room. `all` allows all room-required sessions except labs locked by `lock_labs`. Batches use half of section strength for capacity and affected-student calculations.
- Optimization requires a documented section strength. Older timetable terms without a roster remain fixed or return infeasible when selected as movable; no cohort size is inferred from another term.
- The objective uses one room-on indicator per room/day/period, connected kW, configured period length and tariff, relocation weight, and floor-change weight. CP-SAT uses one worker and the recorded random seed.
- E2 uses `lambda_move` values `[0, 0.1, 0.5, 1, 5]` and `max_relocations` `[0, 5, 10, 20, none]`. Each row is run for every supplied seed.
- E3 closes floors `[5]`, `[4]`, `[4,5]`, and `[5,3]` in turn. E4 marks randomly selected used, eligible rooms unavailable; it compares affected students with a same-seed inferred-only run without failures. The number of runs per `k` equals the number of supplied seeds.
- Timetable room assignments can be inferred and the imported source contains recorded clashes. Fixed source rows are retained; fixed-vs-fixed clashes are reported as unresolvable for a proposal.

## Threats to validity

- Synthetic wattages and the placeholder tariff do not represent metered campus energy or a verified billing schedule.
- Inferred room assignments can alter both the baseline occupancy calculation and the optimizer's movable set.
- The source schedule includes conflicts and possible parser ambiguities; those affect the measured before/after clash counts and the optimizer's feasible space.
- Energy is compared over timetable periods, while utilization uses a six-day denominator; the dataset's flat baseline separately assumes five days at six hours/day.
- No real DSATM meter data, occupancy sensors, or operational room-status history are available.
- Solver time limits may produce `FEASIBLE`, `UNKNOWN`, or `INFEASIBLE`; the generated status and solve time are reported as measured and are not replaced with a presumed optimum.

These experiment notes describe the computation and its limitations only; they do not claim research novelty or interpret results beyond the generated measurements.
